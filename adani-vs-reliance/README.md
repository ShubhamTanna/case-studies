# Adani vs. Reliance: Comparative ESG Analysis

**Reliance edges Adani Enterprises 5–3 on a 10-metric ESG scorecard. It's stronger on net-zero timing, water, safety, gender diversity and CSR scale. Adani leads on emissions disclosure (it reports Scope 3), renewable share and attrition; 2 metrics are even.**

## Question
Measured against the IFC ESG guidelines, which of two Indian conglomerates is the stronger ESG performer, and what should each fix first?

## Data
Public company disclosures: the Adani Enterprises ESG Factbook, Reliance Industries' Business Responsibility & Sustainability Report / Integrated Annual Report (FY2022–23), and the IFC ESG Guidebook as the evaluation framework. Figures are FY23 or FY24, as each company disclosed them.

## Approach
1. Compared the two companies across Environmental, Social and Governance pillars:
   - **Environmental:** carbon emissions, net-zero targets, water, renewable energy
   - **Social:** gender diversity, attrition, health and safety, CSR
   - **Governance:** board structure, transparency and disclosure
2. Benchmarked each area against IFC ESG expectations.
3. Scored 10 like-for-like metrics: winner, loser or even.
4. Turned the scorecard into a verdict for an ESG-focused investor and a priority fix for each company.

## Key findings
| Metric | Adani Enterprises | Reliance Industries | Edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Emissions disclosure | Scope 1, 2 and 3 (FY24) | Scope 1 and 2 only (FY23) | Adani |
| Net-zero target | 2050 (Scope 1 + 2) | 2035 | Reliance |
| Renewable energy | 28% of electricity | 1.4% of total energy | Adani |
| Water | Water-positive target by 2030 | Zero liquid discharge achieved, 90%+ recycled | Reliance |
| Women in workforce | 9.2% | 12.2% | Reliance |
| Women on board | 1 of 6 | 2 of 13 | Even |
| Voluntary attrition | 8.4% | 10.1% | Adani |
| Safety (FY23) | LTIFR 0.13, 6 fatalities | LTIFR 0.1, 4 fatalities | Reliance |
| CSR spend (FY23) | ₹175 Cr | ₹1,271 Cr | Reliance |
| Governance & assurance | Board ESG committees, BRSR, third-party assurance | Board ESG committee, BRSR, independent assurance | Even |

**Scale caveat:** Reliance's Scope 1+2 emissions (37.9M tCO₂e) are about 34× Adani Enterprises' (1.13M tCO₂e), so absolute totals reflect size, not performance, and are not scored.

## Recommendation
- **Investor view:** on current disclosures, Reliance is the lower ESG-risk holding.
- **Reliance:** disclose Scope 3 emissions, and publish a year-by-year renewable pathway. 98.6% of its energy is still non-renewable against a 2035 net-zero goal.
- **Adani:** make zero fatalities a board-level KPI (6 in FY23), and lift women in the workforce from 9.2% towards 12%+.
