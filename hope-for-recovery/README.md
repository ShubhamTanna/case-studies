# Hope for Recovery: Anti-Substance-Abuse Awareness Campaign

**A student awareness campaign on substance abuse: I secured a psychiatrist, Dr. Rohit Deshmukh, and hosted a live expert session for the campaign's Instagram audience.**

Hope for Recovery was a student-led social campaign run by an 11-member team at NMIMS MPSTME (BCVS-II course), February–June 2023. Its goal was to break the stigma around substance abuse and point people and their families to real help.

## What the campaign did

- **Strategy.** The team set objectives and a target audience (people affected by substance abuse and their families), chose platforms (Instagram, Facebook, website) and tactics, and defined success metrics: interactions, event attendance, blog views and people reaching out.
- **21-day content calendar.** It planned a mix of awareness facts, family guides, prevention tips and recovery stories. Each piece was matched to a platform and a format (post, story, reel, live, blog).
- **Expert Instagram Live, 9 March 2023.** Psychiatrist Dr. Rohit Deshmukh discussed substance abuse and its effects on mental health. Before the Live, an "Ask the expert" call collected audience questions.
- **Newsletter** with articles on causes, stages, economic impact, myths vs facts, and a write-up of the expert interview.
- **Website** (hope4recovery.aviant.live) gathering resources, with posts linking to government help services such as the Ministry of Health's Drug De-Addiction Programme.
