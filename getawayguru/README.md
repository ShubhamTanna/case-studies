# GetawayGuru: A Last-Minute Weekend Getaway Platform

**A booking platform concept for spontaneous weekend trips from Mumbai and Pune. A 91-person survey showed that a generous cash-refund policy would quietly subsidise deal-hoppers, so the plan offers free rebooking or travel credit instead.**

## Question
Is there room for a last-minute weekend-getaway platform for young urban travellers, and how should it handle cancellations without bleeding money?

## Data
- **Primary:** an online survey of 91 respondents on spontaneous trips and cancellations, plus initial end-user interviews and feedback on the prototype.
- **Secondary:** market context from Phocuswright (India's travel market had recovered to about 60% of its pre-pandemic size by 2021) and CRISIL (travel operators' revenue growth projected at 12–14% in FY2025).

## Approach
1. Sized the need (the post-pandemic rebound and the rise of short, spontaneous trips) and defined the target user: 22–40, Mumbai/Pune, budget-conscious, impulsive weekend travellers.
2. Surveyed 91 people on where they'd go spontaneously and why they cancel last-minute bookings.
3. Built the concept: last-minute deals, a flexible cancellation policy and curated "hidden gems". Then ran a SWOT, a revenue model and a two-phase funding plan.
4. Designed website wireframes and refined them with user feedback.

## Key findings (survey, n = 91)
| Top spontaneous destinations | Share |
|---|---|
| Lonavala | 69.2% (63) |
| Goa | 51.6% (47) |
| Alibaug | 42.9% (39) |

| Why people cancel last minute | Share |
|---|---|
| Unexpected personal or family reasons | 62.6% (57) |
| Change of plans | 51.6% (47) |
| **Found a better deal** | **30.8% (28)** |
| Work commitments | 22.0% (20) |
| Personal commitments | 18.7% (17) |

Respondents could pick more than one option.

## Recommendation
- **Launch geography:** the Mumbai/Pune drive-to market first (Lonavala, Alibaug), then Goa.
- **Cancellation policy:** almost a third of cancellations come from people who found a better deal. A free cash refund would subsidise exactly that behaviour. Offer free rebooking or travel credit instead, which keeps the flexibility users want without losing the booking revenue.
